Honest and Unpopular Reviews No. 8 Bill & Ted's Excellent Video Game Adventure (NES) This is one of the worst games on the NES. You bounce around time zones grabbing items and there's no logic to any of it. No map, no direction, no hint that you're doing the right thing. Rufus is the only one who tells you anything and it still isn't enough. Then there's the music. A few bars on a loop that never stops. It grinds away the entire time you're wandering around lost, which is the entire time. Playing it muted is an improvement. What gets me is how easy this should have been. Two characters everyone liked, a time machine, and all of history to build levels out of. LJN had everything and did nothing with it. This is one of the games that earned that rainbow its reputation. Nothing about this one is excellent. I rate this one a 1 out of 5.
