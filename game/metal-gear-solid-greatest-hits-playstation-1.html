Honest and Unpopular Reviews No. 29 Metal Gear Solid (PlayStation) Some of the best games I've ever played, I found by accident. I didn't get around to Metal Gear Solid until 2002. There was a Fry's Electronics right behind the apartments I lived in, and I was going through the PS1 section one day when I came across it. It looked interesting so I bought it. It's easily the best game on the PlayStation. Not any of the Final Fantasy games. Not Gran Turismo. It's MGS. Most shooters are typically run and gun. Doom, Quake, and even GoldenEye, you're blasting away. This one, total opposite. You sneak past the guards. Somebody spots you and the alert goes off and you're hiding in a locker until everything quiets back down. It's all on a military base in Alaska. There's a boss at the end of every section and you don't beat any of them the same way you beat the last one. Ocelot, Sniper Wolf, Vulcan Raven. They're all different. The craziest part of this game was Psycho Mantis reading your memory card. He names the Konami games you've been playing. He tells you to set your controller on the floor and then makes it move with the rumble. You can't touch him until you unplug it and put it in the second port. The codec is running the whole time. Campbell, Mei Ling, Naomi, Otacon. That's where the story comes from and it never stops coming. It's also the best Metal Gear game ever made. Better than Snake Eater. It was so good the training missions got released as their own game. Twenty eight years later and nothing in the stealth genre has beaten it. I'll be hiding in my cardboard box if you need me. I rate Metal Gear Solid an outstanding 5 out of 5.
