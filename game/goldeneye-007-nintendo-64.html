Honest and Unpopular Reviews No. 26 GoldenEye 007 (N64) GoldenEye came out in North America 29 years ago today. Growing up, kids from the neighborhood would come over on a Saturday and we'd spend the whole day on local split screen multiplayer. This game caused so many arguments. "Stop looking at my screen!"😂 There might not be a more nostalgic game from the entire decade for me. Shooters on consoles back in the 90s were pretty simple. Get through the level and find the exit. Turok came out on the N64 a few months before this one and that's essentially what it was. GoldenEye had you working through objectives, and playing on 00 Agent piled more of them into levels you'd already cleared. I hadn't played anything that did that up until that point. Shoot a guard in the leg and he grabs it going down. The weapons had zoom. Small stuff, but nobody else was bothering with it. Perfect Dark came out 3 years later. It had bots, so you could get a match going without three other people over at the house. This is the game GoldenEye should have been. GoldenEye is 5th on my all-time N64 list. Perfect Dark is 2nd. The single player hasn't held up the way the memory of it has. Gray hallways and aiming that fights you the whole way. They put it on Xbox a few years back and I gave up on it quickly. A lot of what people love about this one is the Saturday, not the game. I rate GoldenEye a 4.5 out of 5.
