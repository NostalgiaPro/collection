Beyond the timeline

Most accounts in this lane are a feed. This one has a catalogue behind it at thenostalgiapro.com, built from my own collection:

  • The shelf — all 831 games, searchable, by console or by series, with condition on every copy
  • The virtual shelf — photographed wall, clickable section by section
  • The lists — every ranking I've posted, cross-referenced
  • The reviews — the full numbered series with scores
  • Anniversaries — what's having a birthday, drawn from the collection

There's a YouTube channel and a Substack under the same name. X is where the work goes right now.

Who this fits

  • Retro hardware, reproduction parts, display and storage
  • Game preservation and physical media
  • Collectibles, apparel and print aimed at people who grew up in the 80s and 90s
  • Anything where the audience needs to believe the person holding it actually owns it

What I don't do is read a script over a product I've never handled. Everything that goes out is in my voice, about things I have on the shelf. That's the reason the numbers look like they do.
