Privacy policy

For thenostalgiapro.com and the Nostalgia Pro Game Collection Android app. Last updated September 11, 2026.

The short version

This site and app do not collect, store, or share any personal information about you. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no tracking, and no advertising.

What is collected

Nothing. The app is a catalogue of a personal collection. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data, and it has no way to receive them.

What is stored on your device

The app saves a copy of its own pages on your device so it works without an internet connection. If you use the checkboxes to mark games, that also stays on your device. None of it is sent anywhere, and all of it is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Like any web host, those services keep standard server logs that may include an IP address and browser type. Those logs belong to those companies and are subject to their own policies. They are not used to identify anyone and are not available to me in a form that identifies you.

Links to other sites

Some entries link to Wikipedia, YouTube, X, or Substack. Following a link takes you to a service with its own privacy policy, which governs what happens once you are there.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to thenostalgiapro@gmail.com.
