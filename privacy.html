The short version

There are no accounts, no sign-in, no cookies, and no ad networks. I don't ask for your name, email address, or anything else about you. The arcade and the demo game count visits and a few anonymous game moments with GoatCounter, so I can see how many people play. The collection pages count nothing. Your streaks, badges, and high scores stay on your device.

What is counted, and where

I use GoatCounter, a small analytics service that does not use cookies. It only runs on the arcade at /arcade/, its six game pages, and the demo game at /play/. Each count is one small image request to nostalgiapro.goatcounter.com. No third-party script is loaded.

On the arcade hub and each arcade game page, opening the page sends the page's address, its title, and the address of the page you came from, if your browser provides one. When you finish a run of an arcade game, it sends one "game over" event with the game's name. Your score is not sent.

The demo at /play/ doesn't send page views. It sends short anonymous event names for a few moments in the game, like starting it, opening the map, finishing the demo, playing again, or tapping one of its links (for example the feedback form or the merch shop). Each event carries a label for where the game is running, such as this site or itch.io. No score, no name, and nothing you type is sent.

Like any web request, these also reach GoatCounter with your IP address, your browser's user agent, and its language setting. GoatCounter uses them to work out a browser name, operating system, country, and language, and stores only those, as totals. Its privacy policy says it keeps the IP address and user agent in memory for up to 8 hours so a reload isn't counted twice, and never writes them to its database. Screen size is not sent. GoatCounter stores nothing on your device.

What I end up seeing is totals, like how many visits a game page got today, how many runs ended, and which browsers and countries they came from. None of it is tied to you, and I have no way to pick out any one person.

What isn't collected

No accounts, no sign-in, no cookies, no advertising IDs, and no ad networks or ad trackers. I don't collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and nothing is sold or shared.

What is stored on your device

Daily Shelf progress and streaks, badges, arcade high scores, the demo's saved settings and character, your theme and 1996 mode choice, and any games you mark with the checkboxes are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. None of it is sent anywhere. The Android app also saves a copy of the collection pages so they work without an internet connection. All of it is removed if you clear the site's data in your browser, or uninstall the app or clear its data.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Like any web host, those services keep standard server logs that may include an IP address and browser type. Those logs belong to those companies and are subject to their own policies. They are not used to identify anyone and are not available to me in a form that identifies you. The collection pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser fetches those files from Google.

Ads

There are no ad networks here, and nobody pays to appear on this site. The demo has one real ad, for my own Nostalgia Pro merch on its end screen, and its results screen links to the same shop. Every other ad in the demo is a parody.

Links to other sites

The Donate and Merch links go to my shop on Fourthwall, and the feedback form is hosted by Tally. Anything you buy, donate, or type there is handled by those services under their own privacy policies. I read the feedback you send, so only include contact details if you want a reply. Some pages also link to Wikipedia, YouTube, X, or Substack, and some game pages embed YouTube videos in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). The weekly roundup signup opens my Substack page, so the email you enter goes to Substack, not to this site. Once you follow a link, play an embedded video, or submit one of those forms, that service's own privacy policy applies.

How to block counting

GoatCounter ignores the browser's Do Not Track setting, and my counting code doesn't check it either. Most ad and content blockers block goatcounter.com, and if yours does, nothing is counted and every game still works. Turning off JavaScript also stops it, but the games need JavaScript to run. The collection pages never count anything, so you can browse them as usual.

Deleting data

Because the counts are anonymous totals and aren't tied to anyone, there is nothing I can look up or delete for a particular person. To remove what's on your device, clear the site's data in your browser or the app's data in Android settings.

Children

The site and app are not directed at children. They don't ask anyone, including children, for personal information. The anonymous counting described above works the same for everyone.
