THE NINTENDO 64 LIBRARY GOES DEEPER

My thread from X, as posted.

When people talk about the N64 it's usually the same few games that get brought up. The Nintendo 64 turns 30 today in North America. There's a lot more to the library than the games everybody already knows. No Mario 64. No Ocarina. No GoldenEye. No Mario Kart. Everyone played those. Here's the 10 you should play this weekend.🧵
September 26, 2026View on X

1Wave Race 64

The waves actually move in this one, so the same turn can play out different every lap. It came out about a month after the console did. Spend some time in Dolphin Park before you start racing.

September 26, 2026View on X

2Perfect Dark

You needed the Expansion Pak to play most of it. Without one you only got a small piece of the game. The Combat Simulator lets you add computer bots to a match if you don't have three other people to play with.

September 26, 2026View on X

3Star Wars Episode I: Racer

This is the podrace from the movie turned into a full racing game, with Watto's junkyard between races where you spend your winnings on parts. Hold the boost too long and your engines will catch fire, at least until you buy better parts.

September 26, 2026View on X

41080° Snowboarding

Nintendo's snowboarding game from 1998 is slower and heavier than the other snowboarding games from back then. Ice and powder handle differently, so you have to pay attention to what you're riding on.

September 26, 2026View on X

5Pilotwings 64

It launched here the same day as Super Mario 64 but didn't get close to the same attention. Most of the game is flying a hang glider or a rocket belt over Little States, a small version of the U.S. that has Mario's face on Mount Rushmore.

September 26, 2026View on X

6Killer Instinct Gold

This is Rare's home version of Killer Instinct 2. It has the combo breakers and ultras from the arcade, plus a training mode that shows you how to do the combos step by step. The stages are 3D with a camera that moves around during fights.

September 26, 2026View on X

7San Francisco Rush: Extreme Racing

The hills send your car way up in the air. There are shortcuts hidden all over every track that take a lot of runs to find. Crash hard enough and the car blows up, then drops you back on the road a second later.

September 26, 2026View on X

8Duke Nukem: Zero Hour

Eurocom made this one only for the N64 in 1999. It's a third person Duke game where he goes back in time chasing aliens, from the Old West to Victorian London.

September 26, 2026View on X

9WWF No Mercy

This was AKI's last wrestling game on the N64. A lot of wrestling fans still call it the best one ever made. Championship mode changes depending on whether you win or lose your matches, so a title run can go a lot of different ways. People are still making mods for it more than 25 years later.

September 26, 2026View on X

10Star Wars: Rogue Squadron

Factor 5's Star Wars flight game takes place between A New Hope and Empire. On the Hoth mission you trip AT-ATs with the tow cable on the snowspeeder. It was also one of the first games to use the Expansion Pak for higher resolution.

September 26, 2026View on X

That's the ten. Happy 30th to the Nintendo 64.
