THE SEGA GENESIS LIBRARY GOES DEEPER

My thread from X, as posted.

Every console has the same six games people name, and then a whole library underneath that nobody talks about. The Genesis turns 37 today in North America and it has one of the deepest catalog of games any system from that era has. No Sonic. No Streets of Rage. No Mortal Kombat. Everyone played those. Here's the 10 you should play this weekend.🧵
August 14, 2026View on X

1Krusty's Super Fun House

A Simpsons puzzle game where you herd rats through a building, which is the last thing anyone wanted out of a Simpsons license in 1992. That's why it got passed over. It's a legitimately good puzzle platformer and it gets hard fast.

August 14, 2026View on X

2The Tick

Based on the animated series, and it plays nothing like a cartoon tie-in. It's a heavy, slow, mean brawler where you feel every hit, and it came out in 1994 while the show was still on. Most people who loved the cartoon never knew the game existed.

August 14, 2026View on X

3Stargate

Acclaim put out a movie tie-in a full year after the movie left theaters, which is how you kill a game. It's a solid run and gun with real level variety and it deserved better than the release window it got.

August 14, 2026View on X

4Wolverine: Adamantium Rage

The Genesis version and the SNES version are two completely different games made by different teams, and almost everyone with an opinion on this one is talking about the SNES one. The Genesis game is the better of the two. It's obtuse and it does not hold your hand, and the soundtrack is worth the cartridge on its own.

August 14, 2026View on X

5Boogerman: A Pick and Flick Adventure

A 1994 platformer where you attack with boogers and farts, and it controls better than it has any right to. Interplay put real animation and a real soundtrack behind a joke premise. People saw the premise, assumed it was garbage, and never picked it up.

August 14, 2026View on X

6The Strike Trilogy

Desert Strike, Jungle Strike, Urban Strike Everybody played Desert Strike and stopped there. Jungle Strike is the better game, with ground vehicles, bigger missions, and a difficulty curve that respects you. Urban Strike went further and put you on foot inside buildings. EA was making some of the strongest original work on this console and two thirds of it went unplayed.

August 14, 2026View on XDesert Strike: Return to the GulfJungle StrikeUrban Strike

7Batman: Revenge of the Joker

Sunsoft published two completely different Batman games and both are hard to find now. The NES version gets all the attention because of what it sells for. The Genesis one barely gets mentioned at all. Ringler Studios built this port with Tommy Tallarico arranging the music, which on this console means it sounds heavier than it has any business sounding.

August 14, 2026View on X

8X-Men 2: Clone Wars

The best licensed action game on the Genesis, and most people never got to it because the first one had that level where it makes you hit the reset button on the console. Six characters that genuinely play differently. Sega put this out in 1995, when everyone had already looked past the machine.

August 14, 2026View on X

9RoboCop versus The Terminator

The SNES version got cut apart. This one didn't. It's more violent than almost anything else on the console, Mark Miller did the soundtrack, and it's hard enough that most people who owned it in 1993 never saw the end of it.

August 14, 2026View on X

10The Adventures of Batman & Robin

The best looking game on the Sega Genesis and it isn't close. Clockwork Tortoise pulled effects out of this hardware in 1995 that nobody thought were in there, with a Jesper Kyd soundtrack running under all of it. It's also brutally difficult, which is exactly why it never found the audience it earned. If you play one game off this list, play this one.

August 14, 2026View on X

That's the ten. Not a ranking and not a best of. The games that got buried under the ones everybody names. Thirty seven years in and the library is deeper than the same six games people keep repeating.
