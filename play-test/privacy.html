Privacy

Nostalgia Pro: The Game, and its demo. Last updated October 2026.

The short version

No accounts, no sign in, no ad trackers, no cookies. The game never asks for your name, email or location. Your progress stays on your own device.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress and settings in your browser's local storage, under names that start with npgame_. That includes your Saturday or season in progress, your shelf, fridge magnets, tackle box, your character's look, the visit streak date, and settings like volume, text size and effects. None of it is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser's site data for this page erases it.

Anonymous counts

To learn which parts of the game people reach, the game sends small anonymous counts to GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly stats service. A count is just the page or an event name (for example, "reached the swap meet" or "watched a commercial") and which site the game is on (for example, itch.io). The game sends no referrer, no ID, no cookie and nothing about you. GoatCounter says it keeps only totals, not IP addresses.

The counts are off on test copies of the game, and anyone with an ad or tracker blocker simply sends nothing. The game works the same either way.

Links out

A few buttons open other sites in a new tab, like the feedback form, the merch shop and the full game's store page. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Kids

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
